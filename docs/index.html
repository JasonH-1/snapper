Snapper

Film looks, live.

Snapper is an iPhone camera that shows your scene through hand-made film looks before you take the shot: sun-faded Disposable, cinematic Neon Nights and flattering Soft Portrait. What you see is what you save, at full resolution.

  • Swipe the live preview to change looks.
  • Change a photo's look or its strength after you take it.
  • Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no tracking.
